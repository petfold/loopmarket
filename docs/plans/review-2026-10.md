# Review, 2026-10-09

The code, performance and design review of ontodag and loopmarket done on
2026-10-09 is one document, kept in ontodag because most of it concerns
both repositories:
[ontodag `docs/plans/REVIEW_2026-10.md`](https://github.com/petfold/ontodag/blob/main/docs/plans/REVIEW_2026-10.md).

The parts about loopmarket:

- §2.2: the goals against the code. U1–U4 hold. "ontodag is the one
  intersection engine" (`ontodag-coupling.md` §5) is not reflected: the
  solver, the CLI and the auction still match by the give × want product,
  and gate G3's threshold was never measured.
- §3.2: complexity. `cli.py` is 39% of the package's statements; `gate=`, `held=` and
  `available=` are threaded through 20, 13 and 12 signatures; record
  versions are handled at call sites; 43 settings; dead code.
- §4: defects fixed on 2026-10-09 (see CHANGELOG "Unreleased").
- §5: compatibility with ontodag. Offer ids hash ontodag's spellings,
  which can change inside one contract version; offer pins are never
  compared with the installed ontodag; loopmarket uses private ontodag
  and recordstore names; the duration grammars of `loop` and ontodag
  disagree.
- §6: performance. The indexed matcher finds the same matches 8–13×
  faster than the product; `find_loops` after tonight's caching.
- §7: coverage (91% locally; CI skips the chain tests) and the tests to
  add.
- §8: recommendations, including wiring the indexed generator, failing
  closed on pins that differ from the installed ontodag, splitting
  `cli.py`, and revisiting the P3 build order against the README's gates.

The probe that produced the matching numbers is `scripts/perf_matching.py`.
